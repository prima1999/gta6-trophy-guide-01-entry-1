Platinum roadmap

Four stages, in order. Story first, missables as you go, collectibles next, grinds last. Roughly 200 hours for the full list, per community estimates — long, not hard. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals.

Missable warnings — 10 trophies

Community reporting says there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at a separate slot before every trophy flagged MISSABLE, and one at the start of every chapter. Story chapter and mission trophies are unmissable — they pop for finishing — but the ten below are tied to single moments.
